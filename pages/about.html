About us

Who we are and why we do laundry differently.

Our story

Fresh & Fold started in 2019 as a two-machine shopfront. Today we run three outlets and process over 1,200 kilograms of laundry every week, always by hand-checking each order before it is packed.

Our promise

Every order is tracked with a numbered token, so nothing is ever lost or mixed up between customers.

Our team
